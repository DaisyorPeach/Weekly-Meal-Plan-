Monday
Rolled oats with yogurt & banana
Breakfast · 5 mins
⌄
Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 3 cups milk or water
  • 4 individual yogurt pots (Ski / Jalna)
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • Pinch of salt
Method
  1. Combine oats, milk and salt in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Microwave on high for 3 mins, stir halfway through.
  3. Divide into bowls and top with a dollop of yogurt and sliced banana.
Kids' tip: let them add the banana slices themselves — they're more likely to eat it.
Mini pasta bake (thermos lunch)
Lunch · 10 mins · uses leftover bolognese
⌄
Ingredients
  • Leftover bolognese mince sauce
  • 2 cups cooked penne pasta
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Splash of water or stock
Method
  1. The night before: combine leftover mince sauce with cooked pasta and a splash of water to loosen.
  2. Stir through grated cheese.
  3. Heat in a saucepan until piping hot, stirring well.
  4. Pour immediately into pre-heated thermos and seal.
This is the easiest thermos lunch — no extra cooking, just reheating Tuesday's bolognese with pasta and cheese.
Honey soy chicken thighs with rice & broccoli
Dinner · 40 mins · oven
⌄
Ingredients
  • 6–8 chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 cups frozen broccoli
Method
  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Mix soy sauce, honey, oil and garlic powder. Coat chicken thighs in the marinade.
  3. Place on a baking tray. Bake for 35 mins until golden and cooked through.
  4. Cook rice as per packet. In the last 5 mins, microwave broccoli until tender.
  5. Serve chicken over rice with broccoli. Pour any tray juices over as a sauce.
Make extra — leftovers go straight into the thermos for Tuesday's lunch.
Tuesday
Scrambled eggs on toast
Breakfast · 8 mins
⌄
Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • Knob of butter
  • Salt & pepper
  • 4 slices multigrain bread, toasted
Method
  1. Whisk eggs, milk, salt and pepper together.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over low-medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs. Stir gently and slowly with a spatula, removing from heat just before fully set.
  4. Serve immediately on toast.
Low and slow is the key — overcooked scrambled eggs are rubbery.
Pasta bolognese
Dinner · 30 mins
⌄
Ingredients
  • 500g beef mince
  • 2 × 400g tins diced tomatoes
  • 400g spaghetti or penne
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp mixed herbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
  • Grated cheese to serve
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large pan. Brown mince over high heat, breaking it up, about 5–6 mins.
  2. Add garlic powder, herbs, salt and pepper. Stir for 1 min.
  3. Add tinned tomatoes. Simmer uncovered for 15 mins.
  4. Meanwhile, cook pasta as per packet. Drain.
  5. Serve mince sauce over pasta with grated cheese on top.
Make a big batch — save half the mince sauce for Monday's pasta bake lunch.
Wednesday
Savoury chicken rice
Lunch · 20 mins · prep night before
⌄
Ingredients
  • 1½ cups long grain rice
  • 2 chicken thighs, diced small
  • 1 cup frozen peas & corn
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook rice as per packet and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Cook diced chicken until cooked through, about 6–8 mins.
  3. Add frozen veg and stir for 2 mins until heated through.
  4. Add cooked rice and soy sauce. Toss everything together for 2 mins.
  5. Cool, store in containers. Pack hot in pre-heated thermos in the morning.
Pre-heat the thermos with boiling water for 5 mins before filling — food stays warm until school lunch.
Slow cooker curried sausages SC
Dinner · 15 mins prep · 6–8 hrs low
⌄
Ingredients
  • 500g thick sausages, cut into thirds
  • 3 potatoes, peeled & cubed
  • 2 carrots, peeled & sliced
  • 1½ cups chicken or beef stock
  • 400g tin diced tomatoes
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper
  • 2 cups rice, to serve
Method
  1. Place sausage pieces, potato and carrot into the slow cooker.
  2. Mix stock, tinned tomatoes, curry powder, garlic, salt and pepper and pour over.
  3. Cook on LOW 6–8 hrs or HIGH 3–4 hrs.
  4. Cook rice when ready to serve. Ladle curried sausages over rice.
Set it before the school run. Use a slow cooker liner for zero cleanup.
Thursday
Buttered noodles with chicken & peas
Lunch · 15 mins · prep night before
⌄
Ingredients
  • 200g egg or hokkien noodles
  • 1–2 leftover cooked chicken thighs, shredded
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook noodles as per packet. Drain.
  2. In the same pan, melt butter over medium heat. Add peas and cook 2 mins.
  3. Add noodles, shredded chicken and soy sauce. Toss together for 2 mins until heated through.
  4. Cool and store in containers. Reheat thoroughly and pack into pre-heated thermos in the morning.
Hokkien noodles are pre-cooked so just need a quick heat — fastest option for a school night.
Slow cooker chicken curry SC
Dinner · 10 mins prep · 6–8 hrs low
⌄
Ingredients
  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ginger powder (optional)
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1 cup frozen peas or beans (last 30 mins)
  • 2 cups rice, to serve
Method
  1. Place chicken thighs in the slow cooker.
  2. Mix coconut milk, stock, curry powder, garlic, ginger, salt and pepper. Pour over chicken.
  3. Cook on LOW 6–8 hrs or HIGH 3–4 hrs.
  4. Add frozen veg in the last 30 mins.
  5. Shred chicken with two forks in the pot. Serve over rice.
Make extra — this is Friday's thermos lunch. It tastes even better the next day.
Friday
Pasta with tuna & veggies
Dinner · 20 mins · one pot
⌄
Ingredients
  • 400g penne pasta
  • 2 × 185g tins tuna in spring water, drained
  • 1½ cups frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook pasta as per packet. In the last 3 mins, add frozen peas to the boiling water.
  2. Drain, reserving a small cup of pasta water.
  3. Return to pot over low heat. Add butter and toss until melted.
  4. Add drained tuna and stir gently. Add a splash of pasta water if it seems dry.
  5. Season and serve with grated cheese on top.
Peas cook right in the pasta water — one pot, minimal dishes on a Friday night.

⌄
Ingredients
  • 2 × 420g tins baked beans
  • 4 eggs
  • Knob of butter
  • 4–6 slices multigrain bread, toasted

Method

  3. Toast bread. Spoon beans over toast and top with a fried egg each.
Soy noodles with egg & frozen veg
Lunch · 10 mins
⌄
Ingredients
  • 200g hokkien or egg noodles
  • 4 eggs
  • 1½ cups frozen peas or mixed veg
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
Method
  1. Cook noodles as per packet. Drain.
  2. Heat butter in a pan. Add frozen veg and stir for 2 mins.
  3. Add noodles and soy sauce, toss together for 1–2 mins.
  4. In a separate pan, fry eggs to your liking.
  5. Serve noodles in bowls with a fried egg on top each.
Slow cooker sausage & veg casserole SC
Dinner · 10 mins prep · 5–6 hrs low
⌄
Ingredients
  • 500g thick sausages, halved
  • 3 potatoes, peeled & cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1½ cups beef or chicken stock
  • 400g tin diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp mixed herbs
  • 1 cup frozen green beans (last 30 mins)
  • Salt & pepper
  • Bread rolls to serve
Method
  1. Add sausages, potato and carrot to slow cooker.
  2. Mix stock, tinned tomatoes, garlic, herbs, salt and pepper. Pour over.
  3. Cook on LOW 5–6 hrs or HIGH 3 hrs.
  4. Add frozen beans in the last 30 mins.
  5. Serve in bowls with bread rolls to soak up the sauce.
Thick and hearty — not soupy. Mash a few potato pieces into the sauce if you want it thicker. Leftovers are Sunday lunch.
Sunday
Beef fried rice
Dinner · 25 mins · one pan
⌄
Ingredients
  • 500g beef mince
  • 3 cups cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 1½ cups frozen peas & corn
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Heat oil in a large pan or wok over high heat. Brown mince, breaking up, about 5 mins. Drain excess fat.
  2. Push mince to the side. Add frozen veg and stir for 2 mins.
  3. Push everything aside. Crack eggs into the pan and scramble quickly.
  4. Add rice and soy sauce. Toss everything together over high heat for 3–4 mins.
  5. Taste and season with extra soy or pepper as needed.
Cook extra rice on Saturday night and refrigerate it — day-old rice fries up much better than fresh.

Monjaro & eating
Prioritise protein first at every meal before carbs — eggs, meat, yogurt, cheese. Portions will naturally be smaller; that's normal and expected. Save every leftover confidently — they become the next day's thermos lunch with zero extra effort.
Nausea days
On injection day and the day after, stick to gentle options — warm baked beans, a yogurt, microwaved peas, or a warm Milo. Avoid greasy or very rich meals those days. The tuna pasta and yogurt & muesli breakfasts are your best bet.
Thermos lunches for kids
Always pre-heat the thermos first — fill it with boiling water, leave for 5 minutes, then pour it out before filling with food. Rice meals and noodles both hold heat well until school lunch. Hokkien noodles from Woolies are pre-cooked and need just a quick reheat — the easiest option.
Slow cooker timing
Wednesday's curried sausages, Thursday's chicken curry, and Saturday's casserole all go on from midday or earlier on low. Wednesday and Thursday you can set them before the school run. Slow cooker liner bags (near the glad wrap at Woolies) mean cleanup is just lifting the bag out — no scrubbing.
Sausages doing double duty
One 1kg pack of thick sausages covers both Wednesday's curried sausages and Saturday's casserole — split the pack and freeze half if you shop early in the week. Thick beef or pork sausages from Woolies or Coles are around $5–7/kg.
Cutting costs
Frozen veg over fresh wherever possible — identical nutrition, no waste, much cheaper. Use Woolies Essentials or Coles Smart Buy for all tinned goods, pasta, oats, and rice. Chicken thighs are cheaper and far more flavourful than breast for slow cooking — always go thighs.
