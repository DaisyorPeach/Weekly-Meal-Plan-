Monday
Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 3 cups milk or water
  • 4 yogurt pots (Ski / Jalna)
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • Pinch of salt
Method
  1. Combine oats, milk and salt in a microwave-safe bowl.
  2. Microwave on high for 3 mins, stir halfway.
  3. Divide into bowls and top with yogurt and sliced banana.
Kids' tip: let them add the banana slices themselves — they're more likely to eat it.
Ingredients
  • Leftover bolognese mince sauce
  • 2 cups cooked penne pasta
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Splash of water or stock
Method
  1. Night before: mix leftover mince sauce with cooked pasta and a splash of water.
  2. Stir through grated cheese.
  3. Heat in saucepan until piping hot.
  4. Pour immediately into pre-heated thermos.
No extra cooking — just reheating Tuesday's bolognese with pasta and cheese.
Ingredients
  • 6–8 chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 cups frozen broccoli
Method
  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Mix soy sauce, honey, oil and garlic powder. Coat chicken.
  3. Bake on a tray for 35 mins until golden.
  4. Cook rice as per packet. Microwave broccoli in last 5 mins.
  5. Serve chicken over rice with broccoli. Pour tray juices over.
Make extra — leftovers go into the thermos for Tuesday's lunch.
Tuesday
Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • Knob of butter
  • Salt & pepper
  • 4 slices multigrain bread, toasted
Method
  1. Whisk eggs, milk, salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in non-stick pan over low-medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs. Stir slowly, removing from heat just before fully set.
  4. Serve immediately on toast.
Low and slow is the key — overcooked scrambled eggs are rubbery.
Ingredients
  • 500g beef mince
  • 2 × 400g tins diced tomatoes
  • 400g spaghetti or penne
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp mixed herbs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper · grated cheese to serve
Method
  1. Heat oil. Brown mince, breaking up, 5–6 mins.
  2. Add garlic, herbs, salt and pepper. Stir 1 min.
  3. Add tinned tomatoes. Simmer 15 mins.
  4. Cook pasta as per packet. Drain.
  5. Serve sauce over pasta with grated cheese.
Make a big batch — save half the mince for Monday's pasta bake lunch.
Wednesday
Ingredients
  • 1½ cups long grain rice
  • 2 chicken thighs, diced small
  • 1 cup frozen peas & corn
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil · salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook rice as per packet. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil. Cook diced chicken through, 6–8 mins.
  3. Add frozen veg, stir 2 mins.
  4. Add rice and soy sauce. Toss together 2 mins.
  5. Cool, store in containers. Pack hot in pre-heated thermos.
Pre-heat thermos with boiling water for 5 mins first — food stays warm until school lunch.
Ingredients
  • 500g thick sausages, cut into thirds
  • 3 potatoes, peeled & cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 1½ cups chicken or beef stock
  • 400g tin diced tomatoes
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder · salt & pepper
  • 2 cups rice, to serve
Method
  1. Place sausages, potato and carrot in slow cooker.
  2. Mix stock, tomatoes, curry powder, garlic, salt & pepper. Pour over.
  3. Cook LOW 6–8 hrs or HIGH 3–4 hrs.
  4. Serve over cooked rice.
Set it before the school run. Use a slow cooker liner for zero cleanup.
Thursday
Ingredients
  • 200g egg or hokkien noodles
  • 1–2 leftover chicken thighs, shredded
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce · salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook noodles as per packet. Drain.
  2. Melt butter in same pan. Add peas, cook 2 mins.
  3. Add noodles, chicken and soy sauce. Toss 2 mins.
  4. Cool, store, reheat and pack into pre-heated thermos.
Hokkien noodles are pre-cooked so just need a quick heat — fastest school night option.
Ingredients
  • 6 chicken thighs
  • 400ml tin coconut milk
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ginger powder (optional)
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1 cup frozen peas (last 30 mins)
  • 2 cups rice, to serve
Method
  1. Place chicken in slow cooker.
  2. Mix coconut milk, stock, spices, salt & pepper. Pour over.
  3. Cook LOW 6–8 hrs or HIGH 3–4 hrs.
  4. Add frozen veg last 30 mins.
  5. Shred chicken in pot. Serve over rice.
Make extra — this is Friday's thermos lunch. Tastes even better the next day.
Friday
Ingredients
  • 400g penne pasta
  • 2 × 185g tins tuna in spring water, drained
  • 1½ cups frozen peas
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup grated cheese · salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook pasta. Add frozen peas in the last 3 mins.
  2. Drain, reserving a cup of pasta water.
  3. Return to pot. Add butter and toss until melted.
  4. Add tuna, stir gently. Splash pasta water if dry.
  5. Season and serve with grated cheese.
Peas cook right in the pasta water — one pot, minimal dishes on a Friday night.

Sunday
Ingredients
  • 500g beef mince
  • 3 cups cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 1½ cups frozen peas & corn
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tbsp oil
Method
  1. Heat oil in wok over high heat. Brown mince, breaking up, 5 mins.
  2. Push mince aside. Add frozen veg, stir 2 mins.
  3. Push everything aside. Scramble eggs in the pan.
  4. Add rice and soy sauce. Toss everything 3–4 mins over high heat.
  5. Season and serve.
Cook extra rice Saturday night — day-old rice fries up much better than fresh.

Monjaro & eating
Prioritise protein first at every meal before carbs — eggs, meat, yogurt, cheese. Portions will naturally be smaller; that's normal. Save every leftover — they become the next day's thermos lunch with zero extra effort.
Nausea days
On injection day and the day after, stick to gentle options — warm baked beans, a yogurt, microwaved peas, or a warm Milo. Avoid greasy meals those days. Tuna pasta and yogurt & muesli breakfasts are your best bet.
Thermos lunches for kids
Always pre-heat the thermos first — fill with boiling water, leave 5 minutes, pour out, then fill with food. Rice meals and noodles hold heat well until school lunch. Hokkien noodles from Woolies are pre-cooked and just need a quick reheat.
Slow cooker timing
Wednesday's curried sausages, Thursday's chicken curry, and Saturday's casserole all go on from midday or before the school run. Slow cooker liner bags (near the glad wrap at Woolies) mean cleanup is just lifting the bag out — no scrubbing.
Sausages doing double duty
One 1kg pack of thick sausages covers both Wednesday's curried sausages and Saturday's casserole. Split and freeze half if you shop early in the week. Around $5–7/kg at Woolies or Coles.
Cutting costs
Frozen veg over fresh wherever possible — same nutrition, no waste, much cheaper. Use Woolies Essentials or Coles Smart Buy for tinned goods, pasta, oats and rice. Chicken thighs are cheaper and more flavourful than breast for slow cooking — always go thighs.
