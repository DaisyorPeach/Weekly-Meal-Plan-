Tuesday
Ingredients
  • 6 eggs
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • ¾ cup grated cheese
  • Knob of butter
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Whisk eggs, milk, salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in eggs and scatter cheese over the top.
  4. When edges start to set, fold in half and slide onto plate.
  5. Cut into smaller pieces for the kids.
Make individual small ones if the kids prefer — easier for little hands to eat and they think it's fun.
Ingredients
  • 400g penne pasta
  • 2 × 185g tins tuna, drained
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Salt & pepper
Method
  1. Cook penne as per packet. Drain, reserving a splash of pasta water.
  2. Return to pot. Add butter and toss until melted.
  3. Stir through tuna gently, breaking into chunks.
  4. Add cheese and toss. Splash pasta water if dry. Season.
  5. Cool and refrigerate. Reheat and pack into pre-heated thermos in the morning.
Pre-heat thermos with boiling water for 5 mins first so it stays warm until school lunch.
Ingredients
  • 534g basa fillets
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt & pepper
  • Frozen mash (as per packet)
  • 2 cups frozen mixed veg
Method
  1. Pat basa fillets dry. Season with salt, pepper and garlic powder.
  2. Heat butter in a pan over medium-high heat until foaming.
  3. Cook basa 3–4 mins each side until golden. Squeeze lemon over at the end.
  4. Heat frozen mash and microwave veg as per packets.
  5. Serve fish over mash with veg on the side. Spoon pan butter over.
Don't move the fish while it cooks — let it get a golden crust and it'll release naturally when it's ready to flip.
Wednesday
Ingredients
  • 6 thick slices bread
  • 3 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
  • 2 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp cinnamon, mixed
  • Butter for frying
Method
  1. Whisk eggs, milk and vanilla together in a shallow bowl.
  2. Dip each bread slice in the egg mixture, soaking 10 seconds each side.
  3. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat. Cook bread 2–3 mins each side until golden.
  4. Sprinkle cinnamon sugar over immediately while hot.
  5. Serve with a little extra butter or maple syrup.
The egg and milk soak INTO the bread — kids who won't touch fruit absolutely love this. The sugar caramelises as it hits the hot bread.
Ingredients
  • Leftover basa fillets, flaked
  • 200g hokkien or egg noodles
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

  1. Cook noodles as per packet. Drain.

Ingredients
  • 400g marinara mix
  • 400g penne pasta
  • 400g tin diced tomatoes
  • 200ml cream
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Salt & pepper
  • Grated cheese to serve
Method
  1. Cook pasta as per packet. Drain.
  2. Heat butter in a large pan over medium-high. Add marinara mix, cook 3–4 mins until just cooked through.
  3. Add garlic powder, tinned tomatoes and cream. Stir and simmer 5 mins.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Toss through cooked pasta. Serve with grated cheese.
Don't overcook the seafood — 3–4 mins max or it gets rubbery. Add the cream on low heat and don't let it boil hard.
Thursday — Easy Night ⚡
Ingredients
  • Full pack tortellini
  • 400g tin diced tomatoes
  • 150ml cream
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp mixed herbs
  • Salt & pepper
  • Grated cheese to serve
Method
  1. Cook tortellini as per packet — usually 3–4 mins. Drain.
  2. In the same pot, combine tinned tomatoes, cream, garlic and herbs over medium heat. Stir 3–4 mins.
  3. Add tortellini back in and toss gently to coat.
  4. Season and serve with grated cheese on top.
One pot, 15 mins, barely any dishes. The easiest dinner of the week — perfect for a night when nobody wants to cook.
Friday
Ingredients
  • 4–6 eggs
  • 4 slices bread, toasted
  • Butter · salt
Method
  1. Bring a saucepan of water to the boil.
  2. Lower eggs in with a spoon. Boil 6 mins for runny yolk, 8 mins for firm.
  3. Toast bread, butter it, cut into thin finger-width soldiers.
  4. Serve eggs in cups with soldiers on the side for dipping.
Set a timer so you don't forget about the eggs during the morning chaos! Kids love dipping — it makes breakfast feel like a game.
Ingredients
  • 2 scotch steaks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper
  • Frozen mash (as per packet)
  • 2 cups frozen mixed veg
Method
  1. Take steaks out of fridge 10 mins before cooking. Pat dry and season well with salt, pepper and garlic powder.
  2. Heat a pan over high heat until very hot. Add butter.
  3. Cook steaks 3–4 mins each side for medium. Rest on a board for 5 mins — don't skip this!
  4. While steak rests, heat frozen mash and microwave veg as per packets.
  5. Slice steak and serve over mash with veg on the side.
Resting the steak is the secret to juicy meat. Cover loosely with foil while you plate everything else up. The juices redistribute and every slice will be tender.
Saturday
Ingredients
  • 1 packet pikelet mix
  • Butter for frying
  • Butter & maple syrup to serve
Method
  1. Make batter as per packet instructions.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium with a small knob of butter.
  3. Drop spoonfuls of batter — about 2 tbsp each. Cook until bubbles appear on top, then flip.
  4. Cook 1 more minute until golden underneath.
  5. Stack on plates and serve with butter and maple syrup.
Keep finished pikelets warm in the oven on lowest temp while you cook the rest. Kids love doing the bubble-watching and flipping!

Ingredients
  • 540g lamb steaks
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Frozen mash (as per packet)
  • 1 cup frozen green beans
Method
  1. Mix soy, honey and garlic powder. Coat lamb steaks and marinate 10+ mins.
  2. Heat oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook lamb 3–4 mins each side, spooning marinade over as it cooks. Rest 3 mins.
  4. Heat frozen mash and microwave green beans as per packets.
  5. Slice lamb and serve over mash with beans. Pour pan juices over.
The honey caramelises fast — watch the heat once the marinade goes in so it doesn't burn. Medium-high is plenty once it's glazing.
Sunday
Ingredients
  • 2 × 185g tins tuna, well drained
  • 1 cup frozen mash, thawed
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper · oil for frying
Method
  1. Microwave frozen mash 2 mins to thaw. Let cool slightly.
  2. Mix drained tuna, mash, egg, garlic powder, salt and pepper together.
  3. Shape into small patties. Press breadcrumbs onto both sides.
  4. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook 3–4 mins each side until golden and crispy.
  5. Serve with frozen mixed veg on the side.
Drain the tuna really well — press with the back of a spoon. If the mixture is too sticky, refrigerate 15 mins before shaping. Kids almost always love these.
Ingredients
  • 4 burger patties
  • Remaining beef mince, shaped into extra patties
  • Cheese slices
  • Burger buns, toasted in butter
  • Sauce & toppings of choice
Method
  1. Shape any remaining mince into rough balls — no need to be perfect.
  2. Heat a pan over the HIGHEST heat possible.
  3. Add mince ball and immediately smash flat hard with a spatula.
  4. Cook 2–3 mins until edges are very crispy. Flip and add cheese slice immediately.
  5. Cook 1 more min. Toast buns in butter. Assemble and serve hot.
A very hot pan and smashing immediately = crispy caramelised edges. That's the whole secret. Don't press it down again after the first smash or you'll squeeze the juices out.

Marinara mix — use it Wednesday!
Marinara mix is the most perishable protein this week — that's why it's Wednesday dinner. If plans change, pop it in the freezer before it gets close to the use-by date. Defrost in the fridge overnight before cooking.
Monjaro & bigger proteins
This week has some bigger proteins — steak, lamb, fish. On Monjaro your portion will be smaller naturally. Protein first before the mash. Save every leftover — this week especially, they turn into the next day's thermos lunch with almost no effort.
Smash burger secrets
The pan must be genuinely very hot — this is what creates those crispy caramelised edges. Don't crowd the pan; cook in batches. Cheese goes on the moment you flip so it melts perfectly. Don't press after the first smash or you'll squeeze all the juices out.
Resting your steaks & lamb
Both the scotch steaks (Friday) and lamb steaks (Saturday) need to rest 3–5 mins off the heat before slicing. Cover loosely with foil while you plate the mash and veg. This is what makes them juicy rather than dry every time.
Tuna patties tip
Drain the tuna really well — press with the back of a spoon in the strainer. If the mixture is too sticky to shape, refrigerate for 15–20 mins first. Make them small and round so they cook through quickly and the kids can eat them easily.
Thermos lunches this week
Pre-heat the thermos every morning — fill with boiling water, leave 5 mins, pour out, fill with piping hot food. The tuna penne, basa noodles, marinara pasta, tortellini and steak noodles all hold heat beautifully. All prep the night before takes under 10 mins.
